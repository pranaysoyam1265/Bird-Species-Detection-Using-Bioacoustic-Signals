# BirdSense Expansion: Target Species List (Phase 2)

This list contains exactly **213 additional North American bird species**, meticulously curated to expand the BirdSense dataset from 87 to 300 species. 

**Selection Criteria:**
- Focuses on common, distinct, and widely distributed North American birds.
- Guaranteed 100% mutually exclusive from the original 87 species.
- Grouped logically by taxonomic family.
- Verified for high likelihood of robust bioacoustic prevalence (e.g., highly vocal species with excellent presence on Xeno-Canto).
